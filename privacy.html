Privacy

Last updated 15 September 2026

Alpine Engineering Ltd, Bulgaria, runs this site and the accounts named below. This page says what we collect, why, and how to have it removed. Questions go to support@alpine-ltd.com.

This site

The site sets no cookies and runs no analytics. It is served by GitHub Pages, which keeps ordinary server logs (IP address, browser, time) for a short period under GitHub's own policy. Fonts load from Google Fonts, which sees the request for the font file.

The forms

Two forms on the site are Google Forms. They ask for what you type: an email address, what you work on, a company name where the form asks for it, a rating or a note, and your consent where the form asks for it. The answers land in our Google Workspace and are used for one thing each: to reply to you, or to send the newsletter you signed up for. Nothing is sold or shared. To leave the newsletter, reply to any issue with "unsubscribe" or write to the address above.

The booking

The booking link opens a Google Calendar appointment page. Google keeps the name, email and time you enter; so do we, in our calendar, to hold the call. We do not use them for anything else.

Our social accounts

We publish on LinkedIn, YouTube, Facebook and Instagram. A scheduling tool we run on our own machine connects to those platforms through their official interfaces to post to our own accounts and to read the reach of our own posts. It does not collect, store or process data about people who read, follow, comment or message us beyond what the platform itself shows us in the normal course of using it. No visitor of this site is asked to log in through any of those platforms.

Your rights

You can ask what we hold about you, ask for a correction, or ask for deletion. Write to support@alpine-ltd.com. We answer within 30 days.

Data deletion

To delete everything we hold about you, send an email to support@alpine-ltd.com from the address you used with us, with the subject "Delete my data". We remove your form answers, newsletter entry and booking records within 30 days and confirm by email. If you connected to us through Facebook or Instagram, the same email removes any record on our side; the platform keeps its own copy under its own policy.

Changes

When this page changes, the date at the top changes with it.
