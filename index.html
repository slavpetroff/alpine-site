The record

A PR campaign, thirty days, up to a hundred creators, and every story caught the day it appeared.

Before, one person spent part of every day of the campaign walking two feeds for up to a hundred creators, copying what she found into Excel, keeping the formulas straight, and losing every story that expired before she got to it. Stories vanish after a day, so a day missed is gone for good.

Now she hands over three files, the brief, the list and the product sheet. From there the route runs: the missing platforms found, the followers counted at the start and the end, every story caught the day it appears, the uncertain ones set aside for her eye.

The catch: a product in frame with no tag still counts, and no keyword can see it. So those cases are not guessed. They are set aside, and she looks at them herself, on purpose.

Three working days with her, not three months: the build, the test flight on her old campaign, the fixes, the first live run.
