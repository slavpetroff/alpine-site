A client wanted three apps, so I gave the honest cost.

I knew roughly what one of them would cost and how long it would take. Then I added three of them together, plus the development team needed to build different apps, and all the problems that might come up.

I told them straight that it would be an enormous amount of time and money, which most likely they could not afford. And if they could, they needed that picture first, so they could take an informed decision.

I give the people I work with that overview, with my opinion and the trade-offs, and they choose what works for them.

They ran Django just for the Django admin panel.

That surprised me on an ed-tech platform. Django is a really nice framework, I have a lot of experience with it and I like it. I choose a framework by the use case, though, and for what they did, other frameworks could have given their customers and their developers a much better experience.

If the admin panel was the sole reason, it could have been built really quickly. That's what I'd do: pick the framework for the use case, and just build the missing piece.

TikTok video

My long video views were zero until the shorts linked them.

What I'd been missing was the link between the short videos on each platform and the long ones behind them.

Now, whenever I create something, I start with the whole, the long episode, with all the important details. The long one is for the people who might actually buy my work. I keep the short ones short and on point, to get attention, and still link each one to the long one for anyone who wants to go deeper.

Nothing has come from it yet. But the long ones get some views now, where before they had zero.

Before you book a call

Will the posts sound like me?

If a post ever goes out that doesn't sound like you, that's on us and we make it right, and the most you'll ever do is say yes to the ideas, never stand in front of a camera.

How much of my time does it take?

One 40-minute call where we ask and you tell. A yes on the ideas, and a stop on any post.

Whose accounts are they?

The presence we build together lives on your own accounts, so it's yours from the first day and it stays yours if you ever leave.

What if it doesn't work for me?

If it doesn't work for you, you just call us or send a message, and you're out the next day.

Why only three owners?

We keep it to three owners at a time because that's how many we can do well, and when a place opens, the first one on the list hears from us before anyone else.

Will a quiet month still come?

A quiet month still comes now and then, but you're not left waiting on someone to mention your name, because your stories are still out there in people's feeds.
