The work

Sales first, because that is where the money comes from, and then every other department, one at a time, until the whole company moves and only the decisions reach you.

  1. Finding the next clientYou say who the next client should be, and the work runs as a route of steps: the list comes to you, you strike the wrong names, the research takes your list, the first message comes drafted, and nothing is sent that you did not read and change first.
  2. The campaign and the reportMarketing is many routes, the posts, the campaign, the report, and each one runs step by step, with you or your person reading every step, correcting it, and the next step starting from what you corrected.
  3. The client workDelivery is the work you get paid for, and it runs step by step, each step leaving a result your person checks and fixes before the next begins, with the status report and the handover coming out of the same record.
  4. Quotes, invoices, remindersMoney runs step by step, the quote drafted from the work, the invoice from the quote, the reminder from the unpaid one, and each step waits for your correction, so nothing leaves and nothing is paid without you.
  5. Hiring and the first weeksHiring starts with your rule for the role, where to search and who counts, and runs from there as a route: the search done to your rule, the shortlist yours to cut, the onboarding in your own way, nothing moving without your word.
  6. The weekly numbersManagement is the route that reads the others: the week's numbers pulled from every department, laid out the way you read them, the questions marked, and the decision yours, kept on record so the next week starts from it.

The record

A PR campaign, thirty days, up to a hundred creators, and every story caught the day it appeared.

The record is where we show what we have done, as it happened, for a real client, with the part that went wrong kept in.

  1. By handBefore, one person spent part of every day of the campaign walking two feeds for up to a hundred creators, copying what she found into Excel, keeping the formulas straight, and losing every story that expired before she got to it. Stories vanish after a day, so a day missed is gone for good.
  2. NowNow she hands over three files, the brief, the list and the product sheet. From there the route runs: the missing platforms found, the followers counted at the start and the end, every story caught the day it appears, the uncertain ones set aside for her eye.
  3. The catchThe catch: a product in frame with no tag still counts, and no keyword can see it. So those cases are not guessed. They are set aside, and she looks at them herself, on purpose.
  4. Three working days with her, not three months: the build, the test flight on her old campaign, the fixes, the first live run.
