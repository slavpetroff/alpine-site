What nobody tells you

The three uncomfortable truths

I'd rather you hear them from me, before you buy anything from anyone.

  • A readable file is a copyable file. File systems have no "read but don't take". Secrets never live in this structure — only references to your secret manager. That's a design boundary, not a footnote.
  • The structure declares; git and your OS enforce. Anyone who tells you a context method IS your security is selling you a story. The fences live one layer down — and I set them up with you: protected branches, CODEOWNERS, read-only distribution.
  • Human gates don't scale by themselves. Review gates are the trust feature — and the first bottleneck. Every gate ships with an owner, a queue and an SLA, or it becomes a gate people walk around.
