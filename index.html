How an audit runs

Two weeks, fixed price, no surprises.

  1. Scope call (30 min). You describe the system and what hurts. We fix the scope and the price on that call.
  2. Week one: evidence. Read-only access. Every finding is tied to a file and a line — you can check each claim yourself.
  3. Week two: the plan. The playbook (exact pattern per gap) and a backlog ordered by risk-per-euro. Presented to your team, defended under questions.
  4. Your choice after. Execute it in-house, or we implement it. Either way the audit deliverables are yours to keep.
