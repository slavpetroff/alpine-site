# From last week's problem to a post a buyer can read

Pick one problem from last week, something that broke or something a client needed that you had to work out. Answer the five questions below in the words you would use on a call, then join the answers into one short post.

## 1. The problem

What happened, in one or two sentences that a buyer without your background could follow.

## 2. What you tried first

The approach you started with, and why it looked right at the time.

## 3. What failed

Where it went wrong. Keep the detail that made you wince, because that is the part a reader remembers and believes.

## 4. What you do now

The change you made, or the check you have run since.

## 5. The one line

The sentence you would like a buyer to repeat to a colleague. If the post could keep only one line, keep this one.

## Putting it together

Write answers 1 to 4 as a short paragraph each, then end on the one line. Leave the client's name out unless they have said yes. Read it aloud once, and if a sentence sounds like an advert, say it the way you would say it on a call.

## An example

> We moved a client's nightly billing onto a queue, and the first run sent some invoices out twice. The client noticed before we did. Now nothing ships until a dry run matches the previous run's totals, line by line.

The example is made up to show the shape. Your own week will give you a better one.
